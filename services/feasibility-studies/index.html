Stonewynn services

Feasibility studies before you commit capital

A good feasibility study tells you not only whether a project works, but under which assumptions it stops working. Stonewynn tests market demand, concept and financials for real estate, hospitality, industrial and business projects, and documents the result for investors, lenders and boards.

What the service covers

  1. 01Market demand and concept viability assessment
  2. 02Revenue, cost and investment scenario modelling
  3. 03Returns analysis (IRR, NPV, payback)
  4. 04Sensitivity and risk analysis
  5. 05Feasibility reports for investors, lenders and boards
What you receive

A documented basis for assessing commercial viability and investment alternatives.

Who it is for

  • Investors and family offices assessing opportunities
  • Developers preparing financing
  • Corporates evaluating new facilities or markets
