Facility resource planning and operational readiness

Buildings and facilities perform only as well as the resources behind them. Stonewynn assesses what each facility needs in people, space and services, plans for operational readiness before handover and aligns resources over the asset's lifecycle.

What the service covers

  1. 01Assessment of facility needs and resource requirements
  2. 02Staffing, space and service capacity planning
  3. 03Operational readiness before opening or handover
  4. 04Maintenance and lifecycle resource planning
  5. 05Service provider scope and performance frameworks
What you receive

Resource plans aligned with facility needs and operating requirements.

Who it is for

  • Asset owners and property managers
  • Developers preparing handover
  • Operators of labour accommodation, hospitality and commercial assets
